If you've been around the site for a while, you've probably noticed that things look a little different.

This rewrite is a clean reset for `300zxproject.com`. The old site had picked up some baggage over the years, and a lot of small theme-specific pieces needed to be cleaned up or reworked. Instead of continuing to patch around that stack, I rebuilt the site around a cleaner Minimal Mistakes setup.

## What stays the same?

The important part is still the same: the project itself.

This site is still where I post updates about the Z, write-ups on repairs and fabrication, and notes about 3D-printed parts, tools, and experiments that help keep the car on the road.

## What’s next?

Now that the site has a cleaner foundation, I can spend less time fighting layout issues and more time writing. I also have room to improve a few things over time, like archive pages, better content organization, and polishing some of the project resources.

Thanks for sticking around through the rewrite. There’s more coming soon.
